Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Information We Collect

Call Silencer collects the following information:

  • Phone contacts (to identify allowed callers)
  • Call logs (to display silenced call history)
  • Google account info (for sign-in and sync)

How We Use Your Information

  • To silence unknown callers based on your preferences
  • To sync your settings across devices via Firebase
  • To detect and report spam numbers

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase Firestore. We never sell your data to third parties.

Permissions Used

  • READ_CONTACTS - identify allowed contacts
  • READ_CALL_LOG - display silenced call history
  • RECEIVE_SMS - silence unknown SMS notifications
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS - show timer notifications
  • ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY - control Do Not Disturb
